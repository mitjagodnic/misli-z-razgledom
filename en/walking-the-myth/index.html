About the author

“I believe that a good view sometimes needs distance.”

I write stories that try to understand what remains when the world changes. I love cities, cafés, trains, books and conversations that are in no hurry.
More about me
Biography

Uroš Goričan

I was born in 1969. I grew up in Styria, in a settlement that later became Boguizanogu in my first novel.

I have worked in advertising for more than twenty years, mostly as a creative director. I have led the creative teams at Herman & partnerji, Pristop, LUNA\TBWA, Publicis Slovenia and Saatchi & Saatchi, and looked after the communication of some of Slovenia's strongest brands. My work has won several awards.

I was the first Slovenian to complete the master's programme at the Berlin School of Creative Leadership, where I brought together my experience in advertising, content production and media.

Since 2022 I have been a partner at the communications company Dobre zgodbe (Top Stories). As a brand consultant I help companies create and manage their stories, always where storytelling, branding and strategic advice meet.

I also write screenplays. In 2026 Super! založba published my first novel, Boguizanogu, a coming-of-age novel for grown-ups about the last carefree summer of 1980. It was written without artificial intelligence.

On my desk is Walking the Myth, a literary travelogue about Berlin, a city I have been visiting for almost four decades, and a new novel is slowly taking shape.
